Thank you for helping improve AI

Collect clear, varied screenshots that teach models what real apps and websites look like.

Your work helps improve dataset quality, coverage, and consistency. Fun, careful collecting makes a real difference. 🎉

📏 Rules

  1. Collect a strong batch for each category, with about 50 screenshots from mobile apps and 50 from websites in a browser.
  2. Choose 5 to 10 apps or websites for each category.
  3. Capture about 5 to 10 screenshots in each app or website.
  4. Keep the set varied. Try different screen types instead of repeating the same page.
Tip: variety matters. Mix home pages, detail pages, search, listings, settings, carts, profiles, and other useful screen types when they fit the category.

📦 When you are done

Zip the collection and upload it to the local shared repository.

Thanks again for helping make AI quality better, one screenshot at a time. 📷✨
