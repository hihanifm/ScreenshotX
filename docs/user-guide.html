Welcome & thank you

Thanks for helping make AI better, one screenshot at a time. 📷

This quick guide walks you through setup and the two ways to capture. It takes a couple of minutes — then you're collecting.

🔐 First, a few permissions

Android asks for these one at a time. Don't worry — nothing leaves your device. Each one just lets the app do its job. Tap Allow as they come up.

🪟

Display over other apps

Shows the floating capture button on top of whatever app you're in. On the next screen, find this app and turn the toggle on.
📊

Usage access

Lets saved files include the current app's name (like amazon or chrome) in the filename, so each screenshot is easy to trace back to where it came from.
↕️

Accessibility service scroll mode only

Only needed if you turn on Scroll capture. It lets the app scroll the screen for you so it can stitch a long screenshot. Enable it under Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps.
🖼️

Photos & media

Lets the app save screenshots and read back your collections so it can show counts and let you browse.
🔔

Notifications

Capture runs as a foreground service, which Android requires a notification for. It just keeps the capture session alive.
🎥

Record screen

This is how the screenshot is actually taken. Android shows a one-time "Start recording?" prompt — tap Start now. It only captures when you press the button.

🗂️ Set up a collection

Collections are just named folders that keep your screenshots organized. Pick one of the built-in categories or make your own.

  1. Open the app. You'll see the main screen with the setup card and your collections.
  2. Pick a collection from the list, or tap Add Collection, type a name, and save it.
  3. The selected collection is highlighted — everything you capture goes there until you switch.

📸 Capture: simple snap

The default mode. One tap of the floating button grabs whatever's on screen.

  1. Open the app or website you want to capture.
  2. Tap the floating capture button. The screenshot saves to your selected collection.
  3. A little "Saved in {collection}" chip confirms it. If you turned on Ask before saving, you'll get a preview with Yes / No first.
Tip: long-press the floating button to jump back into the app, and drag it anywhere if it's in the way.

📜 Capture: scroll & stitch

For long pages — chats, feeds, articles. The app scrolls for you and stitches the frames into one tall image.

  1. Turn on Scroll capture in the setup card. The first time, you'll be asked to enable the accessibility service.
  2. Tap the floating button for the first frame. A bottom toolbar appears.
  3. Tap ↓ Scroll & capture as many times as you need, then tap ✓ Done (or tap outside the toolbar).
  4. The stitched image saves to your collection — same "Saved in {collection}" confirmation.
Scroll capture stops automatically after a generous number of frames so it never runs forever.
